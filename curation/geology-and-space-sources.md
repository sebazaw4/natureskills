# Rock, mineral, gemstone, and Solar System source images

These are draft palettes. Select colors from the natural specimen and its matrix, or from adjacent layers exposed in the field. The 60/30/10 order describes design roles and may differ from the proportions in the source image. This collection keeps coal seams and lapis lazuli alongside ore specimens for browsing.

For Earth the swatches are ocean, land, and clouds. Uranus and Neptune use Patrick Irwin's Voyager 2 reprocessing published by the University of Oxford. The Sun uses the grayscale white-light intensity image from NASA/SDO HMI.
