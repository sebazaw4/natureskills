Pistachio uses hand-picked areas from two photos supplied in the project chat. The mauve skin sample `#B78D91` was lightened and cooled to `#C49EAA`. The green kernel sample `#AAAD20` was softened to `#AAAD43`. The sand-brown shell accent `#CEA378` was sampled from the second photo.

In the revised palettes, Avocado's flesh green was shifted from `#97A937` to `#88AA42`. Its gray and brown are sampled colors. Carrot uses three sampled colors. Eggplant's two skin samples `#78494F` and `#B5777C` were shifted toward violet. Its olive accent is sampled.

Madeira Cherimoya was sampled from the Anona market photo supplied in the project chat. The three swatches come from the green skin, pale flesh, and dark seed.
