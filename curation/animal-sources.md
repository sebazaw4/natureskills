# Animal palette source notes

The animal board contains 100 drafts. We searched 127 animal entries for photo references, sampled colors near each animal's characteristic tones, then set aside illustrations, weak matches, and closely related color combinations. Most candidates were screened with image sampling rather than an individually hand-masked animal silhouette. The remaining palettes are ready for visual review.

The Blue-ringed Octopus uses `#E8C384` and `#0364F0` from the on-screen swatches and `#75401E` from the interior of a ring in the screenshot. The supplied screenshot is kept outside the repository.
