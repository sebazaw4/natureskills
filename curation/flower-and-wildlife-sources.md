# Flower and wildlife source photographs

This batch adds 120 flower palettes and 58 animal palettes. We searched 187 flowering-plant and 147 animal entries, selected photographs with usable matches to characteristic tones, then favored triples that were distinct from the existing catalog and from each other. For each draft the source is linked below.

Samples were taken from pixels near characteristic colors in the source photographs, with a preference for central areas. Individual flowers and animals were not manually masked. A source can therefore contribute foliage, light or background color. These drafts still need visual approval.
