# Produce photo queue

Choose fresh, clearly identifiable produce in even light. Look for a recognizable dominant hue and two distinct supporting colors in the fruit, flesh, peel, stem, or leaves. Sample those parts deliberately. Avoid washed-out highlights, mixed pixels at the edges, and shadows that flatten a distinctive hue.
